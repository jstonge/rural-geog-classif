## Rural E-Commerce and Emerging Paths Toward Product Renewal: Evidence from Taobao Villages in Zhejiang Province, China

Jia Zhang

University of Melbourne, Australia

Cassandra C. Wang

Zhejiang University, China

Nicholas A. Phelps and Julie T. Miao

<!-- image -->

University of Melbourne, Australia

The role of extraregional linkages established by e-commerce in stimulating industrial renewal has yet to be sufficiently explored. We propose a framework that specifies the renewal of product as improvement in quality and the increase in the types of products, and understands product renewal as conditioned by the strength of extraregional linkages and the local capacity. Drawing on the original survey data on 241 Taobao villages in Zhejiang Province, a multinomial logit model is used to explore the factors influencing product renewal. We find that the improvement to production processes and merchants ' management capabilities significantly contributes to product renewal, whereas the development of logistic industries and the establishment of e-commerce associations accomplishes the opposite. The authors argue that more attention should be paid to improve institutionalization of the Taobao business model to support the upgrading and renewal of local industries, such as improving local government recognition of e-commerce and popularizing e-commerce associations to a wider area. Key Words: e-commerce, extraregional linkages, product renewal, regional industrial change, rural vitalization.

R ecent scholarly work in economic geography has been preoccupied with the conditions that stimulate regional industrial change (Binz, Truffer, and Coenen 2016; Lee 2017; Zhu, Li, and He 2019). Aside from local conditions and processes emphasized in evolutionary economic geography (EEG), such as technological relatedness, branching processes, and combinatorial knowledge dynamics, there is increasing evidence that extraregional linkages also contribute to the renewal and restructuring of regional resource bases and the creation of new regional industrial paths (Andersson, Bjerke, and Karlsson 2013; Zhu, He, and Zhou 2017). Regions are able to supplement locally available resources and knowledge with bodies of knowledge, resources, and capabilities residing elsewhere through building pipelines with other regions, and for that purpose to stay dynamic and innovative (Bathelt, Malmberg, and Maskell 2004; Chen 2015; He, Yan, and Rigby 2018).

Extraregional linkages can be established in various forms, such as market linkages, formal networks, and informal linkages (Trippl, Grillitsch, and Isaksen 2018). The literature on trade networks suggests that the market linkage is a conduit for an inflow of new knowledge and information embodied in the goods from outside regions, which is conducive for processes pertaining to the improvement of existing product lines and renewal and upgrading of industrial sectors (Loof and Andersson 2010; Andersson, Bjerke, and Karlsson 2013). Over the past years, the twin forces of globalization and the information and communications technology revolution are combining to create e-commerce, which has changed traditional ways of business. E-commerce, the digital dimension of the platform economy, opens the configurations of the scope, scale, and density of economic interactions (Richardson 2015). Geographically, e-commerce allows firms to interact with and participate in different locations and networks, and knowledge exchange and interactive learning can occur effectively through e-commerce between spatially distant actors (Amin and Roberts 2008; Richardson 2015). In other words, e-commerce leads to greater information flows and wider market coverage while reducing the dependence of regions on existing resources. We therefore argue that ecommerce can be regarded as one specific means through which regions develop critical external linkages to absorb and transform outside resources and knowledge into a renewal process of local industries (Acquier, Daudigeos, and Pinkse 2017). The role of e-commerce in renewal of local industries is still a relatively unexplored area of research, however.

Taobao villages are prime examples to explore how e-commerce can contribute to the renewal and upgrading of local industries. The rapid development of e-commerce has also brought new opportunities for rural regions with limited availability of local assets. With the advantages of flexible operation and low barriers to entry, Taobao (the biggest e-commerce platform in China) has become popular for many rural entrepreneurs to seek business opportunities. Against this background, the Taobao village, a typical geographical agglomeration of e-commerce activities in rural China, was born in 2009 in Zhejiang Province. Since then, this new economic geography phenomenon has experienced exponential growth across China (Zhang et al. 2022). The number of Taobao villages has risen to 1,437 from just 3 in 2009, and online shops in Taobao villages have created 8.28 million job opportunities (Ali Research 2019). As a result of the adoption of e-commerce, Taobao villages have managed to acquire locally absent resources and build an extensive market network, allowing them to combine the local and nonlocal sources in the product renewal process.

Both the lack of extant literature on the specific effects of the e-commerce business model and the call for new theories of the role of extraregional linkages in industrial change motivate this article. Indeed, both gaps in our understanding push in the direction of inductively generated theory derived from exploratory data analysis methods. Therefore, aside from seeking to address these two empirical and theoretical gaps, the further contribution of this article is to make first use of the large sample of surveyed data from 241 Taobao villages to analyze how e-commerce stimulates the renewal of local industry in a village. Also, considering that the core of e-commerce is the transaction of products, the product renewal process is assumed to reflect the renewal and upgrading of local industry. Meanwhile, we also provide empirical evidence for the new industry development of backward regions. In addition, Taobao villages in Zhejiang Province are chosen as our empirical setting, as Zhejiang Province is in a leading position in the development scale of Taobao villages and the home to the corporation Alibaba (the world ' s largest retailer and e-commerce company).

This article is organized in five sections. Following the introduction, we present the key elements for our theoretical framework and research hypotheses, with special regard to the conditions for e-commerce to stimulate product renewal. This is followed by a clarification of our research site, data sources, and research method. After presenting the spatial distribution of product renewal of Taobao villages in Zhejiang Province, we then present the empirical results from the estimation. The final section summarizes the main conclusions and discusses the implications of the results.

## How E-commerce Stimulates Product Renewal

Literature on global production networks has demonstrated that local actors such as firms and institutions might be able to obtain relevant resources and knowledge complementary to local capabilities by networking with other actors involved in such an international division of labor system (Yeung 2009; Coe, Dicken, and Hess 2008). Yet, its conceptual and methodological approach still has some limitations. First, it is not applicable to rural areas with a low innovation capacity. There is a consensus that the resources available in rural areas are quite ubiquitous and uncompetitive, making it difficult for them to participate in a global production network (Galliano, Goncalves, and Triboulet 2019). Despite a growth in the literature emphasizing global -local relations, suitable ways to build extraregional linkages for rural areas and how they affect the renewal and upgrading of local industries has been little explored. Second, much of the existing literature on extraregional linkages focuses on foreign direct investment (FDI; Bathelt and Li 2014; Y. D. Wang et al. 2016) or foreign trade (Mao and He 2019b). The rise of digital platforms with closely related economic trends, however, implies a new type of interaction relationship with other regions. Compared to FDI, digital platforms not only provide cross-distance resources, but also tap deeply into undeveloped idle resources, which enables connections to be established with consumers or producers at wider geographical scales (Dillahunt and Malone 2015; Sutherland and Jarrahi 2018). Literature on the platform economy is still in its infancy, with almost no research on the role of platform economy business models in the renewal of local industries. Third, extraregional linkages comprise complex networks that involve spatial integration of multiscale geographic units. Empirical research, however, significantly highlights the interactions between particular local areas and those in other countries, ignoring the linkages between other domestic localities (Coenen, Benneworth, and Truffer 2012). For countries with significant regional differences such as China, different tiers and types of regions can also generate continuity and complexity in linkage networks.

The characteristics of e-commerce provide new perspectives on the overcoming of the limitations for business in rural locations. The disintermediation effect of e-commerce enables merchants to purchase from suppliers directly, sell goods to clients directly, and increase controls over their place in the supply chain (Molla and Heeks 2007). At the same time, almost all village merchants are able to bear the low cost of e-commerce, which meets the needs of merchants with low income and weak risk resistance. Therefore, a variety of entrepreneurs in rural areas that were previously limited by space or excluded from traditional avenues have opportunities to reach ever-more distant consumers effectively and enter new consumer markets. In addition, interactions and information circulation between e-merchants and other market participants cover both domestic interregional and cross-border linkages. More important, the success of Taobao villages in China shows that e-commerce is an essential and suitable way for businesses of rural areas to network and interact with external partners (suppliers, customers, public actors, etc.), both in theory and in practice.

As a new and special product of e-commerce in rural areas in China, discussions on the emergence of Taobao villages have generated interest in economic geography (Yu and Cui 2019; C. C. Wang et al. 2021; Tang and Zhu 2020). A flourishing body of work focuses attention on development models of Taobao villages (Wei, Lin, and Zhang 2020), rural transformation wreaked by e-commerce (Lin, Xie, and Lv 2016; C. C. Wang et al. 2021) and the formation of Taobao villages (Qi, Zheng, and Guo 2019). It is less clear, however, how e-commerce stimulates industrial change and what factors can promote the product renewal of Taobao village to maintain the sustainable vitality of the industry. In response to these omissions, this study focuses on the conditions under which Taobao villages can achieve product renewal by building extraregional linkages through the adoption of e-commerce. Of the various conditions of building extraregional market linkages in the literature, two broad themes can be identified: the strength of extraregional linkages and local capacity (Boschma and Iammarino 2009; Boschma 2017; Miguelez and Moreno 2018).

## Strength of Extraregional Linkages

The extraregional linkages constructed by e-commerce connect actors inside and outside regions, and are essential to providing local merchants with the resources they need to pursue their product renewal (Trippl, Grillitsch, and Isaksen 2018). In Taobao villages, e-commerce stimulates local firms to produce and supply new products by delivering effective new demand information from the external market. Conventionally, the cost incurred in access of information is particularly high, especially in developing countries, which is a critical challenge for firms in these regions to find customers and raise their incomes (Tarafdar, Anekal, and Singh 2012). The most basic benefit of e-commerce, though, is access to a larger network of providers, consumers, or resources. In other words, e-commerce enables online merchants to expand local markets into national or even global markets by reducing information costs. As various scholars in this field have confirmed, larger markets increase the incentive to produce new ideas, and hence induce product innovations (Schmookler 1962; Rothwell et al. 1974). At the same time, the demands of customers become more diversified and will become more particular over time. Online merchants therefore must be able to predict the changing preferences of customers to meet market demands and obtain a competitive advantage. Carrying out transactions on e-commerce platforms allows online merchants to collect and store a large amount of information on consumer preferences and behavior data (Ramanathan, Ramanathan, and Hsiao 2012; Sutherland and Jarrahi 2018). For example, the user reviews on rating systems help merchants to judge future product trends, which can reduce their uncertainties about expected profits of new products (Fontana and Guerzoni 2008). A case study of nonfarm rural firms in China demonstrates that businesses that produce and sell products by themselves integrate new information acquired from outside regions into their knowledge base and apply it to design new and innovative products (Q. N. Zhou, Gao, and Chimhowu 2019). Moreover, even those who only sell products but produce nothing can transmit diversified market demand to suppliers.

In summary, extraregional linkages established by e-commerce can effectively stimulate the upgrading and renewal of products. The strength of extraregional linkages can be reasonably considered to be contingent on the degree of e-commerce use by village merchants. The proportion of online merchants and online sales of products in one village can reflect the degree to which Taobao villages use e-commerce. Based on the theoretical backdrop just identified, we hypothesize the following:

Hypothesis 1 : The proportion of online merchants in one village is positively associated with the degree of product renewal.

Hypothesis 2 : The proportion of online sales of products in one village is positively associated with the degree of product renewal.

## Local Capacity

Yet building extraregional linkages with external actors is not a sufficient condition for ensuring the renewal of local industries. A number of studies have shown that local capacities are necessary for a recipient to benefit from building extraregional linkages (Cortinovis and van Oort 2019; Mao and He 2019a). The definition of local capacity refers to a supportive regional environment that contributes to understanding and absorbing external resources for local industries (Binz, Truffer, and Coenen 2016; Boschma 2017). It determines the extent to which the positive external resources brought by extraregional linkages are fixed locally. In this study, we argue that merchants ' related capacity, supporting industries, and enabling institutional environment are considered critically important for improving local capacities (Binz and Anadon 2018; Trippl, Grillitsch, and Isaksen 2018).

Merchants ' Related Capacity. First of all, as for e-commerce merchants ' production capacity, merchants with more advanced technology and largescale production capacity are better able to translate external resources into innovation activities (Tavani, Sharifi, and Ismail 2014; Winkelbach and Walter 2015). In other words, even if the merchant acquires new market information, a low level of production capacity means that do not have the capacity to capitalize on it (Najafi-Tavani et al. 2018). Merchants need to improve product qualities or develop new products to adapt to the changing market information brought by e-commerce (Tzokas et al. 2015), and these products are increasingly nested in new technologies, which necessitates that merchants keep abreast of technological change (Hsieh and Tsai 2007). Advanced production technology enables merchants to develop and reconfigure acquired external knowledge and pioneer in process innovations. Meanwhile, large-scale production capacity produces economies of scale, which is also supposed to stimulate innovation. This is supported by Binz and Anadon (2018) who argued that the generic capabilities in mass manufacturing are put forward as crucial factors in the process of regional industrial renewal. Hence, a sufficient degree of production capacity is required for adapting to external market demands in the process of building extraregional linkages.

Second, the adoption of e-commerce in management is a critical way for merchants to improve management capabilities and stimulate product renewal (Kraemer, Gibbs, and Dedrick 2005). On one hand, product diversification is likely to increase the associated costs such as coordination difficulties and information asymmetry as the firm activities become more complicated and diversified (Gary 2005). Firms thus become less willing or able to innovate products. On the other hand, the collection of customer feedback, systematic employee training, product design, and supplier quality management are basic elements of product quality management (Shan, Zhao, and Hua 2013). It is therefore necessary to reestablish or update the existing effective standardized process for product quality improvement, which requires a flexible and efficient management system of the firm. Molla and Heeks (2007) claimed that e-commerce integrates and coordinates the material, information, and financial flows of all links by capturing, organizing, and sharing key information of each business process, which not only can reduce the management costs of developing new products (Terzi 2011), but also can strengthen the effective and efficient communication and coordination between various segments (Sanders 2008). Therefore, we propose the following hypotheses:

Hypothesis 3 : After the adoption of e-commerce, the improvement to production processes has a positive influence on product renewal in Taobao villages.

Hypothesis 4 : After the adoption of e-commerce, the improvement of merchants ' management capabilities can stimulate the product renewal in Taobao villages.

Supporting Industries. As one of the most important related industries of e-commerce, the logistics and express delivery industry plays an important role in stimulating product renewal in Taobao villages (Ma et al. 2017). More often than not, logistics is viewed as a bridge linking merchants and consumers. Products ordered through e-commerce platforms need to be delivered to the place designated by customers through logistics, and the transportation time should be within the customers ' range (Tang and Zhu 2020). Therefore, the development of logistics industries determines the market scope of online products. Meanwhile, transportation infrastructure will be improved with the development of logistics industry, which may reduce transportation costs. Bernard et al. (2003) emphasized that the high transportation costs have a negative impact on the level of product innovation. Arguably, the reduction of trade costs might increase the possibility of enterprises participating in product renewal activities. Therefore, the specific research hypothesis offered for empirical testing is as follows:

Hypothesis 5 : The development of the logistics industry has a positive influence on product renewal in Taobao villages.

Institutional Environment. Informed by Martin ' s (2010) alternative model of local industrial evolution, the existence of a supportive institutional environment is associated with regional industrial change. Although the government does not intervene in a Taobao village ' s market-oriented business activities and independent decision-making, the interplay of policy interventions has essentially shaped industrial development activities (Qi, Zheng, and Guo 2019; Wei, Lin, and Zhang 2020). The government can provide e-commerce entrepreneurs with more preferential policies in terms of business loans, house leasing, and network fees, which can greatly encourage the entrepreneurial passion and foster more online merchants. Based on the work of Wei, Lin, and Zhang (2020), the government also provides infrastructure guarantees, such as building urban -rural logistics systems and Taobao industrial parks. Moreover, the policy actions in market supervision provide better conditions for industry development. Effective supervision can avoid malicious low-price competition, and encourage merchants to obtain market competitiveness through product renewal. In general, the local capacity of businesses in villages and their ability to transform resources from outside into industrial renewal is shaped by the support and intervention of the local government (Binz, Truffer, and Coenen 2016).

Local capacity is also increased through the establishment of e-commerce association. Unlike traditional industry, the integration of online markets and faster product imitation speed make the competition between online merchants more intense and concentrated. Organizationally, the association provides a strategic network mechanism that sets norms, rules, and processes for competition and cooperation (Merrett, Morgan, and Ville 2008). As can be seen from the case of Junpu village (Zeng and Guo 2016), the e-commerce association could strengthen the external economy of rural e-commerce clusters and avoid the vicious competition caused by product homogenization. Enhancing a dynamic cohesion that shapes merchants ' behaviors is one part of the association ' s advantages; the other part is sharing resources and establishing close cooperation relationships. Essentially, the fundamental purpose of establishing an e-commerce association is to seek the common interests of members and improve product competitiveness of online merchants. Therefore, we develop our Hypotheses 6 and 7:

Hypothesis 6 : The support of government has a positive influence on product renewal of Taobao villages.

Hypothesis 7 : The establishment of an ecommerce association has a positive influence on product renewal of Taobao villages.

## Data and Methodology

## Research Site and Data Collection

To investigate the preceding hypotheses, we draw on a questionnaire survey administered to village officials to explore their perceptions of changes in businesses. Unlike previous studies based on secondary data (e.g., Liu et al. 2020) or original surveys of individual businesses in a small sample of villages (e.g., Lin, Xie, and Lv 2016), this study first uses a large number of primary surveyed data to examine the general effects of e-commerce on rural industrial renewal.

The survey was conducted in Zhejiang Province, where rural e-commerce activities are more active because of the demonstration effect of Alibaba and other e-commerce enterprises. According to the statistics of Ali Research, there were 506 Taobao villages in Zhejiang Province in 2016, on which we based our questionnaire survey. The data on Taobao villages used in this study were collected through a survey on sampled village officials (drawn from the village head, deputy head, secretary, deputy secretary, accountant, or other senior position) conducted by a professional survey company between December 2017 and February 2018. Village officials were interviewed face to face and completed a standard questionnaire of forty-one questions, including question on the impact of Taobao and the basic condition of the village, businesses, product, sales, and so forth. People in these leadership positions are very knowledgeable of the latest developments in their villages and they can provide accurate factual estimates and make informed judgments regarding the extent and direction of business changes and the main effects of e-commerce. Therefore, they are the most suitable candidates for investigation.

We investigated all 506 Taobao villages to avoid error caused by manual selection. We set a survey target of 300 valid questionnaires for the sake of representative results. A total of 186 Taobao villages completed the questionnaire after an initial round of surveying and then we asked the survey company to conduct a second round of research to achieve the goal of 300 completed questionnaires, as shown in Figure 1. The final response rate was 59.2 percent of the total number of Taobao villages in Zhejiang province in 2016. Figure 1 shows that Taobao villages are mainly concentrated in Hangzhou of in northern Zhejiang, Jinhua in central Zhejiang, and Wenzhou and Taizhou of southeastern Zhejiang. Our samples basically covered all the gathering areas of Taobao villages. The final sample in this study consists of 241 Taobao villages, excluding those that provided missing or inconsistent information.

## Variable Description

To accurately measure the villages ' ability to use ecommerce to stimulate product renewal, this research focuses on the dynamic changes of various variables after the adoption of e-commerce. A Likert scale ranging from 1 ( very small change ) to 5 ( very large change ) is used to measure the changes in variables.

To assess product renewal more comprehensively, we measure it from two aspects: changes in quality and types of product following previous work by Bils and Klenow (2001). To distinguish between these two forms of product renewal, we define them as vertical product renewal and horizontal product renewal. Vertical product renewal is measured by the improvement in product quality and horizontal product renewal is measured by the increase in the types of products manufactured by the village after the adoption of e-commerce.

According to the theoretical framework and hypotheses discussed earlier, the explanatory variables can be set up from the proportion of online merchants, the proportion of online sales of products, the improvement to production processes, the improvement of merchants ' management capabilities, the development of the logistics industry, the support of government, and the establishment of e-commerce association. The proportion of online merchants refers to the proportion of online merchants to the total merchants in each village and the proportion of online sales of products refers to the proportion of online sales to the total product sales in each village. Moreover, the development of logistics industries refers to the number of service nodes of express outlets in each village in 2016, which is collected from the official Web site (www.kuaidi.com).

## Product Renewal of Taobao Villages in Zhejiang Province

The maps in Figure 2 visualize the spatial variation in the degree of vertical and horizontal product renewal of Taobao villages and the main product type of each county and district in Zhejiang Province. It is feasible to aggregate product types at the county level as the main product of Taobao villages in a county are essentially the same types of categories.

As for vertical product renewal, villages with high values are concentrated in Lin ' an, Cixi, Yuyao, Wuxing, and Luqiao, which represents more positive impacts on product renewal of these regions after adopting e-commerce. Low-value villages, however, are located in Yiwu, Ruian, and Wenling. At the same time, villages with a high degree of horizontal product renewal are located in Lin ' an, Wuxing, Haining, Yuyao, and Jiaojiang. Interestingly enough, it should be noted that Yiwu, Ruian, and Wenling are also gathering places of Taobao villages with a low value, which suggests that e-commerce has not effectively stimulated both the vertical and horizontal product renewal in Taobao villages of these regions.

Figures also show that the main products in areas with a high value for both vertical and horizontal product renewal are clothing (Wuxing), agricultural products (Lin ' an), and hardware accessories (Yuyao). Wuxing and Yuyao are regions with a solid industrial development foundation, which is beneficial for product renewal. Compared with other agricultural products, the main product in Taobao villages in Lin ' an, nuts, can be stored for a long time and their quality is easy to control, which are suitable for sale through e-commerce platform. Besides, the raw material of nuts can be easily processed into new products while retaining their flavor. On the contrary, the main products in villages with low values are daily necessities (Yiwu) and shoes (Ruian and Wenling). Two main explanations for this result are offered here. On one hand, villages in Yiwu are mostly based on the local marketplace, and most of the products selling in Yiwu are coming from other regions. Although these e-commerce merchants can obtain new product demands from the online platform, these demands might not be effectively conveyed to suppliers and successfully transformed into new products. On the other hand, in our survey, about one-third of the village officials in these places pointed out that the adoption of e-commerce has aggravated the cut-throat price war, and products selling online are usually of low quality and selling at low prices. E-commerce merchants therefore are not focused on the renewal and upgrading of products.

## Factors Influencing Product Renewal in Taobao Villages

## MNL Model Results

To test our hypotheses on promoting product renewal from the strength of extraregional linkages and local capacity in Taobao villages, this article conducts a set of multinomial logit regression analyses, and Table 2 gives us the answer to the research question. In addition, due to the characteristics of simultaneous equations of the MNL model, a likelihood ratio (LR) test is used to test the significance of the estimated coefficients and the results are listed in Table 3. All independent variables are found to be significant according to the test.

## Analysis of the MNL Model for Vertical Product Renewal

As shown in Table 2, in the ' Y ¼ 3 ' group model, when the improvement to production processes is large, the probability of a high degree of vertical product renewal is 48.79 times greater ( p &lt; 0.001) than that of a low degree of vertical product renewal. Therefore, we accept our Hypothesis 3. The RRR of improvement of merchant management ability in Taobao villages switching from very small to large is 11.52 ( p &lt; 0.001), and from very small to medium is 12.15 ( p &lt; 0.001) for performing high vertical product renewal versus low vertical product renewal, so Hypothesis 4 is also accepted. These results show that the improvement to production processes and the improvement of merchants ' management capabilities significantly increase the probability of vertical product renewal.

The RRR for a one-unit increase in the variable ' development of logistic industry ' is 0.99 ( p &lt; 0.01) for performing high vertical product renewal versus low vertical product renewal, which shows the negative correlation between the development of the logistics industry and vertical product renewal. One potential explanation is that the development of the logistics industry is not only related to the e-commerce industry, but also related to the geographical location of the village. As can be seen from Figure 1, a large of number of Taobao villages are located in areas with location advantages. These areas might already have a well-developed external transportation system before becoming Taobao villages. Consequently, there is limited scope for further reductions in transportation costs from e-commerce that might stimulate merchants to upgrade and renew products. Meanwhile, the geographical location of villages also affects rural land use and values (C. C. Wang et al. 2021). In the villages with higher land value, the income of villagers relying on rent collection is higher, and they have less motivation to upgrade products to enhance the competitiveness of online businesses (Space Planning Center of Nanjing University &amp; Ali Research 2018). Thus, Hypothesis 5 is rejected.

Analysis of the MNL Model for Horizontal Product Renewal

What follows from Table 2 is that the improvement to production processes and merchants ' management capabilities contributes to the increase in the types of products produced from Taobao villages. Therefore, we accept our Hypotheses 3 and 4. This indicates that the merchants ' production and management capacities are among the most essential determinants for industrial change in Taobao villages, as they have a vital catalytic role in both vertical and horizontal product renewal. This result is consistent with the spatial distribution of product renewal degree in Figure 2. Taobao villages with a high degree of product renewal are mainly distributed in areas with a favorable manufacturing foundation, where production processes are more advanced and industrial chains are more mature and complete.

Establishing e-commerce associations is associated with a 0.03 ( p &lt; 0.01) decrease in the relative log odds of a high level of horizontal product renewal versus A low level of horizontal product renewal, indicating e-commerce associations have a negative impact on product renewal in Taobao villages. Therefore, Hypothesis 7 is rejected. The importance of this finding for policy is immediate and direct, not least because there remains a vast scope for improving institutionalization of the Taobao business model at the local level: Only 16 Taobao villages among our 241 sampled have established e-commerce associations so far. Village officials in our survey were asked to indicate why there was no e-commerce association. Around 40 percent of them indicated that there was no need for establishing an association and there was an absence of policy support. From our visits and interviews, we determined that many village officials are not familiar with e-commerce, which is new and changing, and they also lack relevant management experience. It is difficult for local government officials who lack work experience and knowledge to understand the importance of e-commerce associations and deal with this extremely complex work.

One must not overlook the fact that the increase in proportion of online merchants and online sales of products does not contribute to product renewal in Taobao villages, and our Hypotheses 1 and 2 are rejected. There is no doubt that Internet-based ecommerce provides a new channel for merchants to obtain market information. In light of the information obtained from our field investigation and mechanisms underlying the way that demand acts on product renewal, we can highlight three points from this result. First, the expansion of markets represents not only the increase of demand for new products, but also the increase in potential consumers of existing products. These online merchants might prefer to expand production of exiting products, rather than serve new submarkets. In regions with a low degree of vertical or horizontal product renewal, 38.2 percent and 50.5 percent of these villages, respectively, still have a high degree of increased sales of products. Second, online merchants vary in their abilities to harness exogenous market information. Most of the e-commerce practitioners in Taobao villages are farmers with a limited ability for processing and analyzing information, so it might be difficult for them to accurately grasp the changing market demands and forecast the direction of product renewal. In addition, customers ' needs sometimes conflict with their price awareness (K. Z. Zhou, Brown, and Dev 2009), and many of them are willing to trade off product novelty or quality for lower price. Therefore, customers ' actual purchase behaviors might not be consistent with their expressed market needs, which will also lead merchants to abandon product renewal strategies.

Interestingly, whereas government support has proven useful in a broad institutionalist body of literature on industrial change, it is not effective in promoting product renewal in Taobao villages, and our Hypothesis 6 is rejected. This can perhaps be explained by the fact that the government suffers from bounded rationality (Jones 2003). Information asymmetry exists between online merchants and the government, and it will continue to increase even as the government ' s ability increases. In addition, the Taobao village is a new economic geographical phenomenon, and local government officials lack knowledge and work experience in e-commerce. Hence, it becomes difficult to guarantee that each decision is made in an entirely rational manner.

## Conclusion

It has been argued that extraregional linkages are essential for industrial change. This article is among the first to empirically explore the prospects for industrial renewal -judged in terms of processes of vertical and horizontal product renewal -in Taobao villages in China from the adoption of e-commerce. The findings from 241 Taobao villages in the Zhejiang Province of China fill an empirical gap that exists at present surrounding determinants of extraregional linkages established by e-commerce for the renewal of local industries in rural China. Based on the characteristics of e-commerce and EEG ' s theorizing of industrial development, we developed the argument that extraregional linkages play a vital role in the renewal of local industries in Taobao villages. In our view, the renewal and upgrading of products is not only dependent on the strength of extraregional linkages, but also on the local capacities to absorb and integrate external resources. Thus, making novel use of two MNL models, we sought to elaborate the factors that influence product renewal in Taobao villages.

First, we find that improvements to production processes and the improvement of merchants ' management capabilities are positively associated with vertical product renewal. The development of express logistics industries, however, has significantly inhibited the improvement of product quality. As for horizontal product renewal, the improvements to production processes and merchants ' management capabilities significantly increase the types of products produced from the villages. The establishment of e-commerce, however, shows a negative effect on horizontal product renewal. In addition, the increase in the proportion of online merchants and online sales of products and the support of government do not produce a significant influence on product renewal.

Second, our analysis revealed the likely importance of the institutionalization of the Taobao business model within processes of industrial renewal. Thus, product renewal is also found in our analysis to be affected specifically by the existence of e-commerce associations. A variety of theories -from development studies (e.g., Schmitz 1995), economic geography (Amin and Thrift 1992; Storper 1995), and political economy (Doner and Schneider 2000) -have asserted the positive role of institutions such as traditional industry associations on economic performance. The policy implications here, however, are clouded by the facts that the positive causal connections between institutionalization and economic development are often assumed (Phelps 1992), the coexistence of traditional industry and new e-commerce platform institutions might be at variance with one another, and these connections might fail to support all businesses where there is significant informality and segmentation of businesses (MorenoMonroy 2012). Indeed, the institutional landscape surrounding the Taobao business model is complex and worthy of further study in itself (C. C. Wang et al. 2021). Our interviews suggest that current ecommerce associations ' pursuit of short-term benefits and the lack of foresight of association leaders have made them unable to play a positive role in stimulating product renewal. Considering the difference between traditional industry associations and Taobao village e-commerce associations (themselves with divided loyalties over different e-commerce platforms), a dominant corporate actor with its own Taobao platform embedded in a diversity of rural and periurban villages, important questions remain about how to ensure the e-commerce associations of Taobao villages play their fullest role in supporting industrial renewal going forward.

Third, our inductively generated findings are important in speaking back to theory regarding the openness of potential industrial renewal associated with the extraregional linkages established by the ecommerce. Indeed, the adoption of e-commerce in rural areas is considered disruptive to industry structures and incumbent businesses (Langley and Leyshon 2017). The process of establishing extraregional linkages of Taobao villages, however, is not simply the adoption of e-commerce. It involves the complexities of the characteristics of those businesses and local conditions. In particular, villages with strong local capacity appear to be more favorable for industrial change. Nevertheless, their emergent properties are something that we can begin to grapple with in the future development of theory in a range of academic disciplines. Indeed, these emergent properties will likely challenge us to think across and synthesize findings and insights from various disciplines.

It goes without saying that the findings presented here leave space for further improvements. We only had limited space to explore the factors influencing the product renewal in the overall Taobao villages in Zhejiang Province. According to Wei, Lin, and Zhang (2020), Taobao villages in Zhejiang Province could be divided into production-oriented and market-oriented villages. Future work should explore the differences in mechanisms of industrial renewal between different types of Taobao villages. Second, because it is difficult to collect large scale village-level data in China, we only chose Zhejiang Province as our research object. Although this study has the largest sample data among the current research on Taobao villages, more empirical analyses based on other provinces or at the national level are needed for a deeper understanding of the general effects of ecommerce on stimulating product renewal. /H17039
